How to choose the right tool

1. Start from your output

Need motion? Look at video generators first. Need stills, posters or game assets? Image models. Need a soundtrack or voiceover? Music and audio tools. This list's category tabs get you there in one click.

2. Control beats raw quality

For production work, controllability matters more than one lucky generation. If you need a character to perform a specific action, look for motion-control workflows — Kling AI Motion Control transfers motion from a reference video with frame-accurate precision.

3. Check the license, then the price

Free tiers are great for testing, but commercial use often needs a paid plan. Open-source models run free on your own GPU — filter by “Open source” above to find them.

FAQ

What is the best AI video generator right now?

It depends on your needs. Sora, Google Veo and Runway lead on overall quality; Kling and Hailuo offer great value with generous free tiers; for controllable, frame-accurate motion from a reference video, Kling AI Motion Control specializes in AI motion control for image-to-video.

Which AI tools on this list are free to use?

Many tools offer free tiers or credits, including Kling, Hailuo, Pika, Bing Image Creator, CapCut, MusicFX, BandLab SongStarter and Fadr. Open-source options like ComfyUI, Wan, HunyuanVideo, Qwen-Image, MusicGen and Upscayl are fully free to run locally.

Which AI image generator handles text inside images best?

Ideogram, Recraft and Qwen-Image are currently the strongest for rendering readable text inside images. Qwen-Image is especially good with Chinese and English typography.

Can I use AI-generated music commercially?

It depends on each service's license and your subscription tier. Services like Soundraw, Loudly and Beatoven.ai are built around royalty-free licensing for commercial use. Always check the current terms before publishing.

How do I add a tool to this list?

This list is open source and hosted on GitHub. Open a pull request or an issue with the tool name, URL, a one-line description and its pricing model.
